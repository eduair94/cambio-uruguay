## Scope and mode

- Primary target: `app/pages/reclamo-sodimac-compra-cancelada.vue`
- Mode: Operate first, Read second.

## Audience and job

- Buyers in Uruguay whose July 2026 Sodimac online orders were confirmed or invoiced and then
  cancelled over a reported price error.
- Decide how strong the evidence is, distinguish reclamo from denuncia, produce an accurate
  individual statement, and reach the correct channel.

## Proof and constraints

- Lead with the MEF price-error criterion and clearly preserve its good-faith exception.
- Treat Sodimac terms and buyer communications as evidence, not as proof of an already-established
  violation.
- Keep all entered facts in-browser; no upload, persistence, menu entry, or collective-claim promise.

## Direction and memorable moment

- “Expediente de bolsillo”: case, evidence, statement, channel.
- The first viewport makes the plausible-price versus obvious-error distinction unavoidable before
  the user reaches the complaint generator.

## Unresolved

- Individual outcomes and any later public response or administrative decision must be updated only
  from verifiable sources.

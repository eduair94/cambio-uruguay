If you are visiting Montevideo, Punta del Este, or Colonia and plan to exchange cash, comparing published rates can help you prepare before contacting an exchange house. Compare the same currency and transaction channel, then check the conditions that apply to your exchange.

[Cambio Uruguay](https://cambio-uruguay.com/en) brings together rates published by more than 40 Uruguayan exchange houses and banks. It lets you inspect several options on one screen. Confirm the applicable rate and transaction conditions with the provider before exchanging money.

## 1. Compare the right side of the quote

The labels **compra** (buy) and **venta** (sell) describe the exchange house's side of the transaction. To buy US dollars with Uruguayan pesos, look at the house's sell price. To hand over dollars and receive pesos, look at its buy price.

For quotes with the same conditions, a lower sell price means fewer pesos paid per dollar; a higher buy price means more pesos received per dollar. Check whether the quote applies to counter service, a transfer, or another channel before comparing it with a different provider.

The [rates section (in Spanish)](https://cambio-uruguay.com/cotizacion) also includes currencies such as the euro, Brazilian real, and Argentine peso. Select the currency you will hand over or receive. A larger number in a different currency's row is not a better quote for the same transaction.

## 2. Find a branch you can reach

The [map](https://cambio-uruguay.com/en/mapa) shows exchange-house branches in Uruguay. Use it to locate a few options, then confirm the address, opening hours, and service conditions directly with the provider.

Include travel time and transport costs in your comparison. The displayed rate is one part of the decision, alongside the amount you plan to exchange and the effort needed to reach a branch.

## 3. Understand the conversion before relying on it

The [currency converter (in Spanish)](https://cambio-uruguay.com/convertir) provides an estimate using available quotes. Check the currencies, quote, and date behind the result before using it for a budget. A calculation does not reserve a rate or confirm transaction costs.

The [glossary (in Spanish)](https://cambio-uruguay.com/glosario) explains terms such as spread and interbank rate. An interbank reference and a counter quote describe different markets.

## 4. Keep ATM withdrawals separate from exchanging cash

Exchanging banknotes at a counter and withdrawing money from an ATM are different operations. The [cash withdrawal guide](https://cambio-uruguay.com/en/retirar-efectivo-uruguay) provides information to help you prepare. Confirm current conditions with your bank or the operator before completing a withdrawal.

## 5. Check the date and available warnings

Rates are collected automatically from the providers' sources. The [status dashboard](https://cambio-uruguay.com/en/estado) shows information about that collection process and available warnings.

A recent row date alone does not prove that its price changed recently or that every branch will apply it to every transaction. Before travelling to a branch, confirm the rate, currency availability, and the total you will hand over and receive, including applicable costs.

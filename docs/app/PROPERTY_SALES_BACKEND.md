# Public residential sale catalogue

The separate `/venta-viviendas-uruguay` directory reads **source advertisements**, not inferred physical homes. Its key is `infocasas-<advert ID>` or `casasweb-<advert ID>` and survives changes in title, price and photographs. No address, shared picture, title, agency or coincident price can merge two adverts. Rental collection ownership and keys are untouched.

## Collection boundary

The existing single-instance `currency-property-opportunities` jobs publish the directory alongside the opportunity snapshots. The daily job reads sources; `--analyze-only` projects existing observations without refreshing their dates or consulting portals. It uses the existing shared flock and requires `APP_MONGO_URI`.

- `propertysalelistings` remains private: original normalized facts and exclusion evidence. Each document preserves its original `firstSeen`. A source's explicit `sold`, `deleted`, inactive status, or reserved sale card sets `retiredAt`; an absent ID in a partial sample does **not**. A later valid active reading clears retirement. The private document is retained.
- `propertysalemetas` has independent `infocasas` and `casasweb` observations. A failed, stale or thin new capture cannot freshen successful earlier coverage.
- `propertysalecatalog` is an explicitly constructed public projection with one row per source advert. No contacts, hidden addresses, source valuations, identity/grouping fields or arbitrary publisher keys cross this boundary. Every text value is cleaned of HTML, scripts, contact links, telephone numbers and email addresses; images must use the source's approved HTTPS media hosts.
- `propertysalecatalogmetas`, key `uy-sales`, counts the actual public source IDs and their own newest readings. Counts mean advertisements, never unique properties or total national supply. `sourceCoverage` is always `partial`.

`PublicSaleListing` and `PublicSaleCatalogMeta` in `classes/propertysales/types.ts` define the wire contract. The Nuxt app maintains its own equivalent types because it is built and deployed separately. Public collection replacement is after all row upserts succeed; private input collapse preserves the previous public catalogue. Replacing individual public documents eliminates obsolete unknown fields rather than retaining them through `$set`.

## Current sources, verified 2026-09-06

**InfoCasas:** public `__NEXT_DATA__` from its residential sale searches, explicit `operation_type_id = 1`. The daily sample keeps the existing budget of 500 pages / 20 minutes and its department and date-position strategy. The source's `showAddress` must be exactly true before its coordinates are retained; even then the map precision is `approximate`. Hidden coordinates in a search payload are discarded. Up to 12 own-advert photos and distinct built, total, land and terrace areas are now retained. Old captures without these fields stay unknown until a real new source reading.

**Casasweb:** separate public sale parser, not a transformed rental row. The search form must explicitly select `drpNegocio=V`, the card URL must identify `VENTA_*_CW<ID>`, and its own price block must say `VENTA`. A monthly rental or seasonal price on the same card cannot become a purchase price. Up to 120 search pages / 6 minutes are read across all 19 departments and only apartments/houses, covering initial pages before deeper pagination. The exact server form state is submitted for next pages. Three consecutive failures end the source. Zero expenses are accepted only if the card explicitly says `sin gastos comunes`; absent bathrooms, locality outside Montevideo, construction size and map coordinates remain unknown. Card areas are **reported**, since Casasweb warns its apartment surfaces may include shared areas. The own-card `Proyecto` and `Renta` badges are published as purchase conditions. `PROPERTY_SALES_CW_ENABLED=0` disables future source reads without removing existing observations. Robots and the public sale form were read successfully; no challenge or restricted route is bypassed.

Every Casasweb public advert now requires **its own full detail page**, including purchase terms, with a 250-detail / 6-minute budget after the search. Card-only rows remain private. The lowest advertised prices and ambiguous currencies are checked first, prioritizing adverts without reusable detail evidence on later runs. This was necessary because CW254589's USD 18,000 card omitted its remaining BHU balance, CW252363 omitted inherited-rights-only sale terms, and CW192963 omitted a MVOTMA balance; those terms are visible in the individual description and the catalogue withholds those adverts.

Currency also needs the own detail: on 2026-09-06 cards CW252456 and CW253748 said pesos while their own headers explicitly said USD for the same 330,000 and 98,000 amounts. A detail must corroborate both `Ref: CW<ID>` and one own sale header with the identical amount. It does not assume all prices are dollars or reuse nearby adverts. True or unresolved peso listings remain private until a suitable source contract is established. The enriched input carries private `saleDetailReadAt`, full cleaned description, own gallery, expenses and room counts. A card refresh retains a recent old full detail only if source identity, title, price, currency, location and known physical counts remain compatible; it preserves the old object and its original observation date, without freshening any field. A changed price requires another own-detail reading. Missing/expired detail evidence is never published.

El País's permission for rental indexing is not extended to sale indexing. Mercado Libre and other sources are not counted unless independently harvested and parsed as sales.

## Data validity and consumer meaning

The public directory uses a rolling **21-day** observation window; opportunity comparisons keep their separate **3-day** window. Source listings stay current only through actual re-reading, not hourly publication. An import time is never presented as a source publication date. A legacy unknown `firstSeen` stays null when refreshed.

Publication withholds explicit unavailability, source test adverts, partial payments/rights, remaining mortgage/public-housing balances, token prices under USD 1,000 equivalent, contradictory source amounts or physical data, and ambiguous multi-property offers. Land/fields misclassified as houses and prices per hectare are withheld. An unverified UI/UR price is not substituted with an unrelated USD field. It does not repair currencies or prices by guessing. Missing fields are null, including common expenses; a structural zero needs the advert's own explicit statement of no monthly expenses, and unusually high monthly bills exceeding 1% of the asking price need literal corroboration. Sale price and monthly expenses stay separate. The same catalogue vetos also run before sales enter opportunity comparisons; the original private identity evidence is retained for valid comparison inputs.

Projects, occupied properties, homes requiring renovation, special layouts and extra purchase costs may be useful purchases: they remain in the directory with explicit `conditions`. Optional parking stays visible as `optional_parking` while `parkingSpaces` becomes null, so an additional purchase is never claimed to be included. These conditions and the original source link must remain visible to the user. Source descriptions are quotations of advert facts, not endorsed promises about investment returns or legal/tax eligibility.

Manual import for review: `sync_property_opportunities.ts --analyze-only --sales-snapshot=<InfoCasas capture> --casasweb-snapshot=<Casasweb capture> --dry-run --report=<output>` performs the same merge and projection without writes. Publication uses the same command without `--dry-run`, under the existing job lock. A source capture is validated against its own source identity, observations and previous successful coverage before saving.

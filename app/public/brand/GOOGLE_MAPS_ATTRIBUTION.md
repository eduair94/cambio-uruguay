# Google Maps attribution

The unmodified gray and white SVGs in this folder are official attribution assets from
[Google Maps Attribution Assets](https://developers.google.com/static/maps/documentation/images/Google_Maps_Attribution_Assets.zip),
retrieved on 2026-09-14. They identify live Google Maps data in the moving directory.

Use them according to [Google's Places display and attribution requirements](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies):
18 px display height, readable contrast, 10 px space above and beside the mark, and 5 px below.
The Google Maps marks remain the property of Google.
